Lumen Home

Lumen Home

E-commerce Website, fictional home decor brand

A calmer storefront and a shorter path from browsing to buying.

Challenge
A cluttered catalogue and a six-step checkout caused shoppers to abandon their baskets.
Approach
Reorganised categories, redesigned product pages and reduced checkout to three clear steps.
Illustrative goal
Faster product discovery and a simpler purchase flow on mobile.
Atlas Freight

Atlas Freight

Website Redesign, fictional logistics company

A dated corporate site rebuilt to explain complex services quickly.

Challenge
Buyers could not tell which freight services applied to them or how to request a quote.
Approach
Restructured the site around customer needs and added a short quote request form with clear next steps.
Illustrative goal
More qualified quote requests and a site that loads quickly on slow connections.
Pulse Clinic

Pulse Clinic

UI/UX and Landing Page, fictional wellness clinic

A reassuring booking experience designed for first-time patients.

Challenge
Phone-only booking left new patients unsure about treatments, prices and availability.
Approach
Designed an accessible landing page with plain-language treatment guides and a simple appointment request flow.

Fewer phone enquiries about basics and more confident online bookings.

Our process

Four stages with a review at the end of each, so nothing surprises you.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We learn your goals, audience and constraints, then agree scope, timeline and success measures.

  2. 2

    Design

    Wireframes and visual design take shape in clickable prototypes you can test and approve.

  3. 3

    Development

    We build responsive, accessible pages and test across devices, browsers and connection speeds.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We deploy, check everything works in the real world and hand over documentation and training.
